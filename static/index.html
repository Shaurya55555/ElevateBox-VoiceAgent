ElevateBox Voice Agent

A voice agent that talks to a small business owner in English, Hindi or Telugu, works out how serious the lead is, and sends a WhatsApp follow-up.

Try it

Talk to the agent from your browser. Allow the microphone when asked. The agent greets you first.

checking...

Tip: use headphones so the agent does not hear itself. You can interrupt it mid-sentence.

Get a phone call instead

Enter your number with country code and the agent will call you.

What happens in a conversation

  1. The agent greets you and asks what you sell and how you sell today.
  2. It asks about volume, budget and timeline, one question at a time.
  3. It classifies the lead as Hot, Warm or Cold.
  4. For a Hot lead it sends a WhatsApp message during the call. After every call it sends a follow-up, and it can book a callback at a time you choose.

How it is built

Vapi runs the call, Deepgram handles speech, Groq runs the conversation, Gemini classifies the lead, and this FastAPI service handles tool calls and WhatsApp. Source and notes: github.com/Shaurya55555/ElevateBox-VoiceAgent.

Known limits: the voices are English-only, so Hindi and Telugu replies sound off. The WhatsApp message needs an approved template outside the 24-hour window.
